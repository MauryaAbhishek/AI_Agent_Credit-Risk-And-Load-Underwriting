# Demo policy: debt-to-income (DTI)
Policy version: demo-policy-v1
Status: development-only, not approved for lending.

- DTI = total monthly debt obligations / gross monthly income.
- For demo tests only, DTI <= 35% is the lower demo band.
- For demo tests only, DTI > 35% and <= 45% is the moderate demo band.
- For demo tests only, DTI > 45% is the elevated demo band.
- DTI alone must never determine a real lending decision.
- Missing, conflicting, or unverifiable information must be referred for review.
